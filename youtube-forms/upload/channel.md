## 제휴 가입 신청서에 붙여넣을 소개문 (영문)
> "Copy My Screen" is a tutorial channel that walks travellers through digital arrival cards and e-visa applications (Malaysia MDAC, Singapore SGAC, Thailand TDAC, Japan VJW, US ESTA, Australia ETA and 60+ more) by recording the real application screen step by step. Viewers watch within 1–3 days of departure, so eSIM, airport transfer and hotel offers placed in the description are highly relevant. We publish daily in Korean and English, with Hindi, Spanish, Indonesian and Japanese versions following. Audience: outbound travellers from Korea, India, Indonesia, the US and Australia.
