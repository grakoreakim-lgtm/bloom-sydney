FAQ

Questions from facility managers

What areas do you service?

The Hills District, Parramatta, Ryde, Macquarie Park and the North Shore, with Sydney CBD available on Grand and custom plans. We group visits by day and area so we can keep prices low.

When do you visit?

Usually before you open (7–9 AM) on a fixed weekday that suits you. We'll agree access details with your reception or facilities team at the first setup.

How does the 50% first service work?

Your first visit is half price and you're only charged after the setup is done. The next day we email you a one-click "keep going / pause / cancel" link. If you don't respond, we simply continue at the regular price. One trial per business (ABN).

How can we pay?

Save a card online (charged after each visit), receive a 14-day tax invoice by email (card or bank transfer), or pay by card on our terminal when we visit. All prices include GST and we issue tax invoices with our ABN.

Is it suitable for hospitals and clinics?

Yes. We use low-pollen, scent-free stems for healthcare and aged-care clients, avoid lilies and strong fragrances, and wash vessels every visit.

Can we pause over Christmas or school holidays?

Of course — pause from the link in any visit email or just tell us. You're never charged for a visit we don't make.

Who owns the vases?

We supply and maintain all vessels free of charge. If you cancel, we collect them on our last visit.
